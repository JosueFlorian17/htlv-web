About the network

Unifying global expertise to eliminate HTLV.

The International Research and Innovation Network on HTLV (RIII-HTLV) serves as the premier global infrastructure for academic collaboration, fostering breakthrough discoveries in retrovirology through institutional synergy.

Our Mission

To accelerate the eradication of HTLV-associated diseases by bridging the gap between clinical research and community health across four continents. We leverage shared data repositories and international mentoring to empower the next generation of researchers.

public Global Reach
science Open Science

Core Vision

A world where HTLV infection is effectively prevented, and those living with the virus have access to curative therapies developed through unified global research.

  • check_circle Eliminating research silos
