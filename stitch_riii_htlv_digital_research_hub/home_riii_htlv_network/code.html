About the Network

Advancing Global HTLV Scientific Discovery

The RIII-HTLV is an interdisciplinary platform dedicated to fostering global cooperation between leading virologists, clinicians, and researchers. Our mission is to accelerate innovative research strategies for Human T-cell Lymphotropic Virus (HTLV).

By leveraging collective expertise and high-quality data sharing, we bridge the gap between bench science and clinical outcomes, ensuring that academic breakthroughs translate into life-saving innovations for patients across the globe.

"Collaboration is the key to solving complex virological challenges."

— Global Executive Board
